About the data-centre figures. A data centre has two different megawatt numbers. IT load is the power its computers use. Grid connection capacity is the most it may draw from the network, which also covers cooling, losses and spare headroom. They are not the same, and this map never mixes them. DSIT estimates about 1.8 GW of IT load across roughly 330 UK data centres. DESNZ meter data shows GB data centres drew 4.5 TWh from the grid in 2024, about 2% of grid consumption. NESO's 2026 ten-year outlook expects 5.4 TWh in 2026, 14.6 TWh in 2031 and 23.5 TWh in 2036, because sites rarely run flat out. Over 70 GW of data-centre connection requests are queued, and NESO says close to half may be speculative or duplicated. The Government's 6 GW target for 2030 does not say which measure it means. A queued request is not a commitment.
