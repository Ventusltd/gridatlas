# Civil infrastructure feasibility SEEDS — GridAtlas research entry

Status: design proposal only. [Canonical SEED contracts](https://github.com/Ventusltd/seed-data/blob/main/11_LEGAL_ECONOMIC_INFRASTRUCTURE_SEEDS.md). GridAtlas may supply authorised or fictional geometry and typed asset identity for a future screening instrument; geography is not permission, proximity is not electrical connectivity, and neither implies grid headroom. Keep legal, economic, power-flow and geographical evidence in independent modules with explicit units, version and uncertainty. Initial experiment uses synthetic aggregate civilian cases; no sensitive site-specific access, real routing or operational optimisation. No new layer or release; live composition 202611222343 and STATE remain unchanged. Existing GridAtlas railway browser proof and selective-retrieval source identities are evidence for reuse, not proof of legal or commercial feasibility. Test the bounded hypothetical composition independently before any admission or application integration.
